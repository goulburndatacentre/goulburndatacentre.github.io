Power Infrastructure & Development Pathway

A dual-tier strategy enabling staged energisation from initial site requirements up to ultra-scale transmission delivery.

330 kV Transmission Proximity (Desktop Assessment)

The long-term energy pathway for multi-hundred megawatt hyperscale and AI compute workloads.

  • Proximity: Desktop assessment indicates active 330 kV transmission infrastructure located approximately 6–10 km from site.
  • Scale Potential: Positioned to support future dedicated terminal substation infrastructure subject to Transgrid network studies.
  • Process: Requires formal Connection Enquiry, System Impact Studies, and long-lead transmission planning.

Nearby 33 kV Distribution Network

Targeted for potential early-stage energisation, auxiliary construction power, and operational support.

  • Local Grid: Adjacency to Essential Energy 33kV distribution network (including feeder GOU42 assets).
  • Auxiliary Use: Potential early-stage or auxiliary infrastructure support.
  • BESS Potential: Potential BESS integration opportunity, subject to grid and development studies.

Water & Cooling Strategy

Investigating multi-source water and water-efficient cooling options for modern high-density AI nodes.

Water-Efficient & Liquid Cooling Focus

Primary focus on high-density GPU compute architectures designed for minimal water footprint.

  • Evaluation of closed-loop liquid cooling (CDU / Direct-to-Chip) systems.
  • Prioritizing low WUE (Water Usage Effectiveness) and optimized PUE operational profiles.

Municipal Utility Sourcing

Investigating multi-source water and water-efficient cooling options in collaboration with local authorities.

  • Goulburn benefits from established municipal water infrastructure and regional resources.
  • Exploring recycled water and secondary supply options for facility HVAC and auxiliary cooling needs.
  • All servicing details subject to formal municipal utility capacity assessments.
