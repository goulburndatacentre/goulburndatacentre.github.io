Why Goulburn, NSW?

Strategic geographic location connecting Western Sydney Hyperscale Zones with ACT Government Secure Cloud Hubs.

Regional Support

Power Infrastructure & Development Pathway

A dual-tier power strategy tailored for staged deployment scaling toward ultra-high-density compute workloads.

330 kV Transmission Connection Pathway

Targeted for large-scale, multi-hundred megawatt to gigawatt scale compute infrastructure.

  • Proximity: Approximately 6–10 km from active operating 330 kV transmission infrastructure.
  • Scale Potential: Positioned to support dedicated terminal substation infrastructure for hyperscale platforms.
  • Process: Requires formal grid connection studies, system impact assessments, and Transgrid network engagement.

33 kV Distribution Network Synergy

Suitable for early-stage site energisation, auxiliary services, or modular deployment.

  • Local Grid: Adjacency to Essential Energy 33kV network (including feeder GOU42 assets).
  • Proximity: Near Goulburn local Zone Substation infrastructure.
  • BESS Opportunity: Ideal baseline for integrated battery energy storage system (BESS) co-location.

Water & Cooling Strategy

Engineered for high-density AI/HPC clusters with a focus on liquid cooling efficiency and environmental stewardship.

High-Density Liquid Cooling Focus

Evaluation centered on low-water or zero-water heat rejection systems required for high-TDP GPU nodes.

  • Focus on closed-loop liquid cooling (CDU / Direct-to-Chip) architectures.
  • Minimizing evaporative water consumption to optimize PUE and WUE metrics.

Municipal & Non-Potable Resources

Investigating multi-source water infrastructure to guarantee operational reliability.

  • Goulburn has established municipal water infrastructure and regional resources.
  • Potential integration of recycled or non-potable water for secondary heat rejection.
  • Final servicing capacity subject to detailed engineering and utility assessments.
