Power Infrastructure Strategy

Focusing on major 330 kV high-voltage transmission lines to support multi-hundred MW expansion.

Collector–Marulan 330 kV Transmission Corridor

Primary target corridor for high-capacity, multi-hundred megawatt grid connection.

  • Proximity: Desktop assessment indicates active 330 kV transmission infrastructure located ~6 km from site.
  • Campus Pathway: Potential pathway for high-capacity connection, subject to Transgrid connection capacity and network augmentation.
  • Connection Process: Requires formal Connection Enquiry, System Impact Studies (SIS), and long-lead transmission planning.

Yass–Marulan 330 kV Transmission Corridor

Secondary high-voltage transmission route offering regional grid redundancy potential.

  • Proximity: Desktop assessment indicates secondary 330 kV line located ~10 km from site.
  • Redundancy Target: Potential for dual-feed or staged capacity expansion across the regional network.
  • BESS Integration: Opportunities for integrated large-scale battery storage to support grid stability.
