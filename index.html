Energy Infrastructure & Proximity

Evaluating dual-tier power pathway options across local distribution and regional transmission grids.

  • 330 kV Transmission: Approx. 6–10 km from operating 330 kV transmission infrastructure.
  • 33 kV Distribution: Adjacent to Essential Energy 33kV network (GOU42 feeder) & Goulburn local substation assets.
  • Renewable Synergy: Located within high-density regional solar/wind generation zones with potential BESS integration.
* Grid connection capacity & allocation subject to formal network connection studies.
🌐

Telecommunications Connectivity

Existing and nearby infrastructure providing a robust basis for investigating redundant fibre routes.

  • On-Site Conduit: Existing 33mm optical conduit infrastructure available on site boundary.
  • Corridor Carriers: Nearby Telstra, Optus, Vocus, and NBN fibre assets along major road corridors.
  • Strategic Location: Situated in the Sydney–Canberra corridor with direct access to regional transport routes.
* Specific bandwidth capacities & diverse routing subject to final carrier confirmation.

Water & Cooling Strategy

Designed to support sustainable, high-density AI/HPC workloads with water-efficient cooling architecture.

💧

Water-Efficient Digital Infrastructure Evaluation

The project is being evaluated with a explicit focus on water-efficient cooling technologies to minimize environmental footprint and municipal water demand:

  • Advanced Cooling Architecture: Focused on closed-loop liquid cooling and low-water heat rejection systems tailored for high-density GPUs.
  • Municipal Servicing: Goulburn has established municipal water infrastructure and regional water resources. Final servicing capacity will be subject to detailed engineering assessment.
  • Recycled Water Integration: Potential integration of recycled or non-potable water sources will be investigated for future supplementary cooling requirements.
